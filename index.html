SHEET A.01 — ABOUT

About Me

I am a Quantity Surveyor / Estimator with a Civil Engineering background, working across construction and specialist lighting packages. My work centres on turning drawings, specifications and project requirements into structured quantities, cost estimates and tender-ready commercial documentation.

I support quantity take-offs, BOQ preparation, cost estimation, tender preparation, supplier and subcontractor coordination, procurement support, technical document review, commercial documentation and general project coordination — with experience supporting projects in Singapore, Indonesia, Vietnam, Maldives, Seychelles and other Southeast Asian markets.

I combine engineering knowledge with practical estimating and tendering experience to translate drawings, specifications, and project requirements into structured quantities, cost estimates, and commercially useful tender information.

SHEET A.02 — SERVICES

What I Can Support On Your Project

Estimating and commercial support at any stage — from an early quantity take-off through to final tender submission.

01

Quantity Take-Offs

Detailed measurement and quantification from architectural, structural and MEP drawings, lighting layouts, shop drawings, specifications and schedules.

  • Organised by drawing, area or floor
  • Organised by zone, system or material
  • Organised by item type for pricing
02

BOQ Preparation

Structured Bills of Quantities built for clarity and ease of pricing.

  • Item descriptions, units & quantities
  • Rates, amounts & trade breakdowns
  • Material and labour components where applicable
03

Cost Estimation

Detailed estimates that reflect the real cost drivers of a package.

  • Material, labour & equipment costs
  • Installation & freight
  • Allowances, mark-ups & contingencies where required
04

Tender & Quotation Preparation

End-to-end tender support, from document review through to final pricing.

  • Tender document review & scope identification
  • Supplier quotation collection & pricing comparison
  • Clarifications, tender summaries & final quotation
05

Procurement & Supplier Coordination

Sourcing and commercial comparison across materials and specialist equipment.

  • Materials, lighting fixtures & LED systems
  • Drivers & accessories
  • Installation equipment & subcontractor services
06

Value Engineering

Identifying practical opportunities to improve cost efficiency without compromising intent.

  • Comparing alternative materials & suppliers
  • Optimising specifications
  • Identifying equivalent products
07

Commercial Documentation

Clear, structured documents that project teams can review and trace.

  • Cost summaries & comparison sheets
  • Tender summaries & quotation documents
  • Scope breakdowns & clarification logs
08

Specialist Lighting Packages

Estimating and commercial support specifically for architectural and specialist lighting scopes.

  • Quantity take-off & specification review
  • Supplier comparison & pricing
  • Tender submission support

SHEET A.06 — WORK PROCESS

My Estimating Process

A consistent, traceable process from first document review through to submission.

01

Understand the Scope

Establish the project context, package boundaries and what the client needs from the estimate.

02

Review Drawings & Specifications

Work through architectural, structural, MEP and lighting drawings alongside specifications and schedules.

03

Perform Quantity Take-Off

Measure and quantify items systematically, organised by area, system or item type.

04

Prepare BOQ

Structure quantities into a clear Bill of Quantities ready for pricing.

05

Obtain & Compare Supplier Pricing

Collect quotations and compare price, lead time, compliance and terms.

06

Build Project Costs

Develop the full cost picture across materials, labour, installation and logistics.

07

Review & Validate

Check quantities, rates, exclusions and assumptions before anything goes out.

08

Submit Tender / Quotation

Prepare and issue the final pricing with supporting commercial documentation.
